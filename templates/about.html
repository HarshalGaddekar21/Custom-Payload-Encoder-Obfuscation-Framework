PayloadShield Lab
Custom Payload Encoder & Obfuscation Framework

PayloadShield Lab is an educational cybersecurity research framework designed to analyze controlled payload transformations and evaluate simulated signature-based detection behavior.

The framework provides a controlled laboratory environment for studying encoding, obfuscation, transformation analysis, evasion testing, and comparative detection behavior.

Project Objectives
1
Study Payload Transformations

Analyze how controlled transformations affect the representation of test strings.

2
Evaluate Simulated Detection

Compare original and transformed representations against controlled signature patterns.

3
Perform Defensive Analysis

Examine transformation indicators, entropy, risk levels, and detection scores.

4
Compare Multiple Techniques

Compare the simulated detection performance of multiple transformation techniques.
